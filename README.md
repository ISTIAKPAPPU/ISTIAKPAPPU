### About

I am an XR software engineer and Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Missouri, with 5+ years of Unity and C# experience designing, building and shipping AR, VR and MR applications, live mobile games and multiplayer games. My current work is real-time XR systems that bring AI into live headset sessions, and research on how to make them secure, private and comfortable for the people using them. That work has led to 9 peer-reviewed papers and an IEEE ISMAR 2025 Best Poster Award.

### Selected work

**[Adversarial VR](https://github.com/dependable-cps/Adversarial-VR-Testbed)**: an open-source Unity testbed for evaluating the adversarial robustness of VR cybersickness detection (IEEE ISMAR-Adjunct 2025).

**XR task assistants on Magic Leap 2**: assistants that guide people through a Rubik's Cube solve and a cooking task with spatially anchored overlays and voice cues. [Demos](https://istiakpappu.github.io/portfolio/#work)

**Wynn Slots**: player-facing features and game modes across the Unity/C# client and Java backend services of a live casino-style title. [Site](https://wynnslot.com/)

**Air Hockey Glow HD**: a physics-based arcade game with local multiplayer, shipped to the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/air-hockey-glow-hd-ultimate-2d/id1549140064) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.air.hockey.glow.hd).

**VR games and training**: VR sports games and a large-scale, multi-stage VR training simulation. [Videos](https://istiakpappu.github.io/portfolio/#work)

### Tools I work with

Unity, C#, C++, C, Java, Python, PyTorch, OpenXR, Meta Quest, Microsoft HoloLens, Magic Leap, HTC Vive, Android, Photon and Mirror multiplayer, Git.
